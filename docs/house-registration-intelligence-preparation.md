# PataSpace House Registration Intelligence and System Preparation

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–5A. It does not redesign House Registration, and it does not implement matching, verification, ranking, payments, reviews, notifications, AI Admin Assistant operations or Platform Health Monitor operations.

## Objective

Every successfully registered residential property is now automatically prepared for approved PataSpace systems without requesting duplicate information from the Property Owner, Property Manager or Leasing Agent.

## Prepared systems

Each residential registration receives a behind-the-scenes preparation structure for:

- House Match
- Vacancy Verification
- Search Priority
- Smart Rotation
- Match Results
- Unlock This Listing
- Verified Access
- Viewing Workflow
- Property Reviews
- Notifications
- AI Admin Assistant
- Platform Health Monitor

## House Match preparation

Residential registration data is organised into matchable attributes covering category, location, rent, water information, nearby places and property information. No additional information is requested during preparation.

## Vacancy preparation

If vacant units exist, every vacant unit becomes prepared for future Daily Vacancy Confirmation and future customer-search eligibility. If no vacancies exist, the property remains registered and search visibility is suppressed until vacancies are published.

## Search Priority preparation

The structure prepares ranking signals for verified vacancy status, active vacancy confirmations, freshness of vacancy information and other approved search priority rules. Ranking itself is not performed in this prompt.

## Smart Rotation and Match Result preparation

Residential listings are prepared for future Smart Rotation, including the approved threshold where more than twenty matching properties may use high-quality batches. Match Results preparation includes approved batch behaviour, Unlock This Listing, Verified Access and Smart Rotation participation.

## Unlock This Listing and Verified Access preparation

Every residential property inherits a category-based pricing key from the official PataSpace pricing model. Manual pricing entry is not allowed during registration. Verified Access recommendation rules are prepared but not executed in this prompt.

## Viewing, reviews and notifications

Every residential property becomes eligible for future Viewing Workflow, Property Reviews and approved notification workflows. These systems are not implemented here; only their registration structure is prepared.

## AI Admin Assistant and Platform Health Monitor preparation

New residential properties are recognised for future Platform Admin support. The AI Admin Assistant remains invisible to the registrant and never replaces the Platform Admin. The Platform Health Monitor receives preparation signals for vacancy confirmation activity, verification activity, search demand analysis and other approved recommendations.

## Continuity

Future residential workflows must build on this preparation layer and must not require duplicate residential registration.
