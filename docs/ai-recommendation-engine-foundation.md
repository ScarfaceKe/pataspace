# PataSpace AI Recommendation Engine, Match Scoring and Search Intelligence

This module completes the Search Optimisation and AI Match Engine foundation for Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls.

## AI Match Score

Every matched property can receive an internal AI Match Score. This score remains invisible to customers and exists only to help rank properties accurately. It primarily reflects how closely a property satisfies the customer's selected filters and AI Search Description.

## Match Accuracy First

The AI never scores properties based only on popularity. The highest score should belong to the property that best matches the customer's actual requirements. Additional signals refine ranking only after Match Accuracy has been determined.

## Recommendation Explanation

The engine prepares simple explanations such as matching budget, area, property type, bedroom requirement, parking, water, electricity, security, accessibility and details from the customer's description. Reasons are generated only from actual search requirements and must never be invented.

## AI Description Interpretation

The AI interprets the customer's written description while preserving the original text. It can extract preferences such as quiet neighbourhood, reliable water, access toward JKIA and family-friendly environment.

## Continuous Match Optimisation

The foundation prepares monitoring for match accuracy, search quality, filter effectiveness, recommendation quality and customer interaction with search results.

## Smart Search Recovery

When exact matches cannot be found, recovery searches every estate and neighbourhood in the requested town, then every supported area in the requested location, and only then suggests nearby towns. The AI never skips directly to distant locations.

## Saved Search Intelligence and Notifications

Saved searches are monitored quietly. Customers are notified only when a high-quality, available, verified and currently vacant match appears. Notifications avoid poor matches, duplicates, minor improvements and repetitive listings.

## Recommendation Diversity

When many similar properties qualify, the AI prepares for a healthy variety of suitable options while still respecting Match Accuracy.

## Privacy and Learning

Customer searches, saved searches and AI interpretation remain private. AI may improve interpretation, explanations, search recovery and recommendation quality, but it must never modify Founder-approved ranking priorities, Unlock rules, Verified Access rules, Customer Access Control, pricing or business rules.
