# PataSpace Event Hall Match Intelligence and Customer Experience

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–14A and enforces the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard.

## Objective

Event Hall Match Intelligence ranks, explains, recommends and presents hall results in a simple customer experience while maintaining fairness for Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents.

## Best Match Ranking

Successful Hall Match searches are ranked using approved signals: booking availability, verification status, freshness of availability confirmation, customer preference match quality, hall capacity and previously approved Search Priority rules.

The ranking favours accurate, trustworthy and highly relevant event halls.

## Limited Result Batch

Regardless of how many halls match, customers receive only the approved limited batch. The platform avoids overwhelming customers with long result lists.

## Smart Rotation

When many qualified halls satisfy the same search criteria, Smart Rotation prepares different high-quality halls for future searches while respecting Search Priority rules. Customers should not notice the rotation happening.

## Why This Hall Matches

Each hall card includes simple explanations such as matching the selected location, hall capacity, booking budget, nearby places and booking availability.

## Summary

Each card includes a clearly labelled **Summary**. The summary uses only available listing information and never invents or assumes details.

## Customer Access Control

Hall Match cards enforce the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard. Before access, public hall cards display only the two cover photos, Property Summary, clearly labelled Summary, Why This Hall Matches, Unlock This Listing and eligible Verified Access recommendation.

Before access, the platform does not display contact phone numbers, WhatsApp, Request Viewing or property photos beyond the two cover photos.

After Unlock This Listing or Verified Access is purchased, all property photos, contact phone number, call, WhatsApp and Request Viewing through PataSpace may be unlocked.

## Unlock This Listing and Verified Access

Every hall card includes the official hall Unlock pricing key. Manual price entry is not used.

When only one or two suitable halls are available, the experience prioritises Unlock This Listing. When several suitable halls are available within the approved batch, Verified Access is recommended while individual hall unlocking remains available.

## Hall-specific reviews

If a customer books a hall through PataSpace, review preparation follows the hall-specific rule: the review invitation becomes available after the event has taken place, not after one month.

## Exclusions

Water Information, Electricity Information and Daily Vacancy Confirmation remain excluded from Event Hall matching.
