# PataSpace Office Match Intelligence and Customer Experience

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–13A, including the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard.

## Objective

Office Match Intelligence ranks, explains, recommends and presents office results in a simple customer experience while maintaining fairness for Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents.

## Best Match Ranking

Successful Office Match searches are ranked using approved signals: active vacancy confirmation, verification status, freshness of vacancy confirmation, customer preference match quality, road visibility and previously approved Search Priority rules.

The ranking favours accurate, trustworthy and highly relevant office listings.

## Limited Result Batch

Regardless of how many offices match, customers receive only the approved limited batch. The platform avoids overwhelming customers with long result lists.

## Smart Rotation

When many qualified offices satisfy the same search criteria, Smart Rotation prepares different high-quality offices for future searches while respecting Search Priority rules. Customers should not notice the rotation happening.

## Why This Office Matches

Each office card includes simple explanations such as matching the selected location, road visibility, rent range, nearby places, utility preferences and active vacancy confirmation.

## Summary

Each card includes a clearly labelled **Summary**. The summary uses only available listing information and never invents or assumes details.

## Customer Access Control

Office Match cards enforce the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard. Before access, public office cards display only the two cover photos, Property Summary, clearly labelled Summary, Why This Office Matches, Unlock This Listing and eligible Verified Access recommendation.

Before access, the platform does not display contact phone numbers, WhatsApp, Request Viewing or property photos beyond the two cover photos.

After Unlock This Listing or Verified Access is purchased, all property photos, contact phone number, call, WhatsApp and Request Viewing through PataSpace may be unlocked.

## Unlock This Listing and Verified Access

Every office card includes the official office Unlock pricing key for the office category. Manual price entry is not used.

When only one or two suitable offices are available, the experience prioritises Unlock This Listing. When several suitable offices are available within the approved batch, Verified Access is recommended while individual office unlocking remains available.

## Search Behaviour

The system supports the approved principle that a small number of relevant offices is better than no results when suitable alternatives exist.
