# PataSpace Platform Analytics and Business Intelligence Foundation

This module establishes the central analytics engine for PataSpace. It collects meaningful platform events quietly in the background without affecting customer experience.

## Objective

Analytics helps the Founder understand platform growth, customer behaviour, property performance, revenue performance, operational efficiency and market trends.

## Coverage

Analytics covers Authentication, Property Registration, Property Verification, House Match, Shop Match, Office Match, Event Hall Match, Unlock This Listing, Verified Access, Payment System, Viewing Workflow, Reviews & Ratings, Notifications, Customer Dashboard, AI Admin Assistant and Founder Dashboard.

## Philosophy

Analytics collects only information that improves customer experience, property discovery, platform reliability, Founder decision-making, operational efficiency and revenue growth. Duplicate or unnecessary analytics are not collected.

## Event tracking

The foundation supports customer events, property events, payment events, viewing events, review events, performance events, operational events, AI events and Founder events.

## Performance metrics

The foundation measures platform response times, search performance, Match Engine performance, verification performance, payment performance, notification delivery and viewing workflow performance.

## Property, customer and geographic analytics

The analytics engine prepares property analytics for registration growth, verification rates, vacancy trends, occupancy trends, availability and popularity. Customer behaviour analytics cover search frequency, favourite categories, viewing requests, unlock purchases, Verified Access usage and review participation. Geographic analytics cover counties, towns, estates and neighbourhoods.

## Operational and AI analytics

Operational analytics support verification workload, Daily Vacancy Confirmation compliance, support workload, AI operations workload, payment reliability and notification reliability. AI analytics support trends, bottlenecks, unusual behaviour, growth opportunities and areas requiring Founder attention.

## Data quality and privacy

Analytics enforces accurate collection, duplicate prevention, consistent reporting, reliable calculations and secure storage. Personal information is redacted by default and only authorised administrative purposes may access sensitive context.
