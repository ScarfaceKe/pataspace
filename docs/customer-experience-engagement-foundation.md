# PataSpace Customer Experience and Engagement Foundation

This module establishes the customer experience foundation for Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls.

## Customer Home Screen

After sign-in, customers first see: **What would you like us to help you find today?** They choose Find a Home, Find a Shop, Find an Office or Find an Event Hall to launch the correct AI Match workflow.

## Search Journey

The journey uses intelligent toggle filters, AI Search Description and the correct category-specific Match Engine. The experience remains simple and low-effort.

## Dashboard Organisation

The Customer Dashboard remains accessible through navigation and organises activity into Saved Searches, Saved Properties, Recently Viewed, Active Unlock This Listing, Active Verified Access, Viewing Requests, Notifications and Settings.

## Saved and Recently Viewed

Saved properties never bypass Customer Access Control. Recently Viewed includes public cards and previously unlocked properties. When access expires, properties return to public information only.

## Active Access

Active Unlock This Listing and Active Verified Access display remaining time countdowns that can update automatically.

## Smart Search Recovery

When repeated searches fail, AI can quietly ask whether the customer wants PataSpace to continue searching and notify them when a matching property becomes available. Acceptance creates a Saved Search monitored by the AI Personal Property Assistant.

## Minimal AI Guidance

The AI may provide subtle guidance like increasing budget slightly or expanding to nearby estates. It must not interrupt or dominate the experience.

## Viewing Feedback

After a scheduled viewing, customers can answer whether the viewing was successful. This improves match quality, property quality, AI recommendations and analytics.

## Security

Customer history, saved searches, saved properties, recently viewed properties and active purchases remain private to the account owner.
