# PataSpace Founder Administration Dashboard

This module extends Master Prompts 1–21B. It implements the Founder command centre for overseeing PataSpace operations with AI Operations Officer support.

## Founder Authority

The Founder is the highest authority in PataSpace. Only the Founder may change business rules, Unlock This Listing pricing, Verified Access pricing, AI operational behaviour, platform policies, administrator permissions, critical platform decisions, analytics access and system-wide settings.

No AI process may override Founder decisions.

## AI Operations Officer

The AI Operations Officer acts as the Founder’s operational team, supporting the work normally handled by verification, customer support, finance and content moderation staff. AI always operates under Founder supervision.

## AI Authority Levels

Level 1 allows AI to handle routine operations silently, such as notification retries, receipt regeneration, routine monitoring, spam filtering, minor support, health checks and optimisation.

Level 2 allows AI to investigate and recommend for sensitive matters such as property verification decisions, suspicious payments, reported reviews, potential fraud, disputed viewings and verification inconsistencies.

Level 3 reserves Founder-exclusive decisions for business rules, pricing, Founder-approved workflows, policies, AI rules, security permissions, permanent bans, platform-wide configuration and Founder Blueprint decisions.

## Dashboard Home

The Founder Dashboard provides a real-time overview of active users, registered properties, verified properties, pending verifications, active Unlock purchases, active Verified Access purchases, viewing requests, reviews awaiting attention, Daily Vacancy Confirmation compliance and platform health status.

## AI Summary Panel

The dashboard displays an AI Summary Panel with operational insights such as properties awaiting verification, possible duplicate properties, payment success rate, notification delivery performance, review moderation trends, high customer activity, vacancy confirmation status and platform health observations.

## Founder Approval Centre

The Founder Approval Centre presents AI recommendations requiring approval. Cases include property verification recommendations, suspicious payment investigations, reported reviews, potential fraud, security alerts and exceptional disputes. Each case includes summary, evidence, confidence level and recommended action.

## Search and Quick Access

Founder search can locate customers, property owners, property managers, leasing agents, properties, payments, viewing requests, reviews and notifications, with direct management paths prepared for detailed workspaces.

## Audit Trail and Security

All important Founder actions are securely recorded, including approvals, rejections, pricing changes, business rule changes, security actions and manual overrides. Founder-exclusive controls require the highest platform security, and AI cannot bypass Founder permissions.
