# PataSpace Complete Viewing Workflow

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–16B and preserves Customer Access Control, Unlock This Listing and Verified Access rules.

## Eligibility

Only customers with active entitlement may request a viewing. Eligible customers have either purchased Unlock This Listing for the selected property or unit, or have active Verified Access covering that property. Customers without access cannot submit viewing requests.

## Request Viewing

After premium access is granted, a customer can request viewing through a guided workflow with a date picker, time selector and optional note field. The platform automatically includes customer details, property or unit identifier, category and request timestamp.

## Delivery and response

Viewing requests are delivered to the responsible contact: Property Owner, Property Manager or Leasing Agent. The contact may accept, suggest a different date or time, or decline. Status changes are prepared for customer notification.

## Statuses

Every viewing has one clear status: Pending, Accepted, Rescheduled, Declined, Cancelled or Completed.

## Rescheduling and cancellation

Either party may request rescheduling through the platform. The updated proposal is stored and, once accepted, the schedule updates. Customers and responsible contacts may cancel when necessary, with an optional reason and notification preparation.

## Reminders

The system prepares reminders for both customer and responsible contact 24 hours before viewing and 2 hours before viewing.

## Completion and review preparation

After the scheduled time, the responsible contact can mark the viewing as completed and the customer can confirm whether it took place. If responses differ, both are recorded for future review.

Review invitation is prepared only after completed viewing. Event Halls keep their special rule where reviews become available after the event has taken place.

## Availability and security

Before confirming a viewing, the platform checks that the property is still available, verified or verification-eligible, and eligible for viewing. Only authorised parties may view or manage a viewing request. The platform keeps a complete audit trail.

## History

Customers can access their viewing history. Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents can access viewing history for properties they manage.

## Customer Home Screen Standard

After a customer signs in, the first screen is not their dashboard or previous activity. The first screen asks: **What would you like us to help you find today?**

The customer then chooses one Founder-approved property category:

- 🏠 Find a Home
- 🏪 Find a Shop
- 🏢 Find an Office
- 🎉 Find an Event Hall

Selecting a category immediately opens the appropriate AI Match workflow with toggle-based filters and the AI Search Description section. Customer history and account features remain available inside the Customer Dashboard through cards or tabs such as Saved Searches, Saved Properties, Recently Viewed, Active Unlock This Listing, Active Verified Access, Viewing Requests, Notifications and Settings.

No future prompt should replace the signed-in customer home screen with a dashboard, activity feed or history page.
