# PataSpace Communication and Notification Foundation

This module establishes the platform-wide communication standard for Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls.

## Philosophy

PataSpace notifies users only when necessary, never spams users, prioritises meaningful communication, combines related notifications where appropriate, respects the Invisible Intelligence Principle, and keeps communication simple, timely and relevant.

## Channels

Standard notifications use In-App Notifications. Critical notifications use In-App Notifications and WhatsApp Notifications. Email notifications are not used.

Customers may configure whether they receive notifications for New Matching Properties and Viewing Reminders, but they do not choose delivery channels.

## Property contact notifications

Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents receive notifications for New Viewing Request, Daily Vacancy Confirmation Reminder and Property Became Inactive. They are not notified for every unlock, every Verified Access purchase, every customer search, every property view or every minor platform event.

## Founder notifications

Routine platform operations remain invisible to the Founder when AI manages them. Founder notifications are reserved for critical platform incidents, security incidents, outages, critical payment failures, AI investigations requiring approval and Founder-approved critical events. Critical Founder notifications use Founder Dashboard and WhatsApp.

## Customer communication

Communication features remain locked until Unlock This Listing or Verified Access is purchased. After access, customers may call, start WhatsApp or request viewing.

## WhatsApp preparation

When WhatsApp is selected, PataSpace prepares an editable message containing property category, property summary, property reference or unit identifier, a polite introduction and a request for information or viewing where appropriate.

## Priority and summaries

Notifications are classified as high, medium or low priority. AI may group related notifications into an intelligent PataSpace Update summary. If there is nothing meaningful to communicate, no notification is sent.

## Security

Notifications and communication must never expose protected contact information, premium property information or restricted platform data before Customer Access Control requirements are satisfied.
