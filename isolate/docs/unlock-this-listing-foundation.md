# PataSpace Unlock This Listing Foundation

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–14B and enforces the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard.

## Objective

Unlock This Listing allows a customer to purchase access to one specific property or unit without purchasing Verified Access. The customer always understands exactly what they are buying.

## Coverage

Unlock This Listing applies to Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls. No property type may bypass this workflow.

## Central pricing

All Unlock This Listing and Verified Access prices are centrally managed by PataSpace. Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents cannot configure prices.

## Unit-based unlocking

Unlock This Listing applies only to the specific property or vacant unit selected by the customer, such as House A2, Shop B5, Office K7 or Hall 1. Unlocking one unit does not unlock other units in the same property.

## Before unlock

Before payment, the public card follows Customer Access Control. Customers may see only the two selected cover photos, Property Summary, clearly labelled Summary, Why This Property Matches, Unlock This Listing price and eligible Verified Access recommendation.

No contact numbers, WhatsApp, Request Viewing, extra photos or premium listing information are exposed before access.

## After successful unlock

After successful payment, the selected unit unlocks all uploaded photos, contact phone number, call action, WhatsApp, Request Viewing and premium property information. The customer should not need to refresh the application.

## Ownership and availability after unlock

PataSpace remembers purchased access. While access is valid, the customer is not asked to buy access to the same property or unit again. The card should display Unlocked.

If a vacancy later becomes unavailable, access history is preserved and purchased information remains accessible, while similar matching properties can be recommended by Match Engines.

## Payment scope

Payment processing, transaction history, receipts, payment retries, refunds and payment security are implemented in Master Prompt 15B. This foundation prepares the business rules and access records.

## Shop Commercial Unit Type and Pricing Category

For shops, Commercial Unit Type and Pricing Category are separate. Commercial Unit Type describes what the space is, such as kiosk, stall, showroom, warehouse, godown, boutique or restaurant/café space, and is used for search, filtering, matching and display.

Pricing Category is used only to apply the Founder-approved Small Shop, Medium Shop or Large Shop Unlock This Listing and Verified Access prices. Commercial Unit Type never determines pricing.

## Unlock Access Remaining Time

Unlock records support an unlock expiry date and remaining unlock time display for the Customer Dashboard. When an unlock expires, premium access is removed, contact details and additional photos are hidden, Request Viewing is disabled, and the record remains visible as **Unlock Expired** so customers understand their purchase history.

## Founder-approved access validity

Unlock This Listing grants premium access to one specific property or vacant unit for exactly **24 Hours**, beginning immediately after successful payment. This duration is a permanent platform-wide business rule unless explicitly revised by the Founder.

When the 24-hour period expires, PataSpace automatically removes premium access, hides protected contact information, hides premium property photos, disables Request Viewing unless another valid entitlement exists, and marks the record as **Unlock Expired** while preserving purchase history.
