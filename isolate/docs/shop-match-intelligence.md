# PataSpace Shop Match Intelligence and Customer Experience

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–12A. It does not redesign the Shop Match Engine Foundation.

## Objective

Shop Match Intelligence ranks, explains, recommends and presents commercial shop results in a simple customer experience while maintaining fairness for Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents.

## Best Match Ranking

Successful Shop Match searches are ranked using approved signals: active vacancy confirmation, verification status, freshness of vacancy confirmation, customer preference match quality, business suitability, road visibility and previously approved Search Priority rules.

The ranking favours accurate, trustworthy and highly relevant listings.

## Limited Result Batch

Regardless of how many shops match, customers receive only the approved limited batch. The platform avoids overwhelming customers with extremely long result lists.

## Smart Rotation

When many qualified shops satisfy the same search criteria, Smart Rotation prepares different high-quality shops for future searches while respecting Search Priority rules. Customers should not notice the rotation happening.

## Why This Shop Matches

Each shop card includes simple explanations such as matching the selected business type, location, road visibility, rent range, nearby facilities and utility preferences.

## AI Summary

Each card includes a clearly labelled **AI Summary**. The summary uses only available listing information and never invents or assumes details.

## Property Card Preparation

Every returned shop result prepares property photos, property summary, AI Summary, Why This Shop Matches, Unlock This Listing, Verified Access, Request Viewing, Call Property Manager or Leasing Agent and WhatsApp Property Manager or Leasing Agent.

## Unlock This Listing and Verified Access

Every shop card includes the official commercial Unlock pricing key for that shop category. Manual price entry is not used.

When only one or two suitable shops are available, the experience prioritises Unlock This Listing. When several suitable shops are available within the approved batch, Verified Access is recommended while individual shop unlocking remains available.

## Search Behaviour

The system supports the approved principle that a small number of relevant shops is better than no results when suitable alternatives exist. Business Suitability and Road Visibility remain primary commercial matching factors throughout the experience.

## Customer Access Control

Shop Match cards inherit the platform-wide Customer Access Control Standard. Before access, the card may show summary information, AI Summary, Why This Shop Matches, Unlock This Listing and Verified Access recommendation only. Contact details, WhatsApp and Request Viewing are unlocked only after Unlock This Listing or Verified Access is purchased.

## Commercial Unit Type and Pricing Category

Shop Match Intelligence keeps Commercial Unit Type and Pricing Category separate. Commercial Unit Type helps customers understand and match the nature of the space. Pricing Category is used only for centrally managed Unlock This Listing and Verified Access pricing.
