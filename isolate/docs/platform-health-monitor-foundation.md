# PataSpace Platform Health Monitor Foundation and Business Opportunity Intelligence

This module extends Master Prompts 1–23C. It establishes the Platform Health Monitor and Business Opportunity Intelligence Engine.

## Platform Health Philosophy

The Platform Health Monitor keeps the platform stable, detects operational issues early, recovers from minor failures automatically, protects customer experience and supports Founder decision-making. It remains invisible to customers.

## Health Scores

The system calculates an Overall Platform Health Score supported by Server Health, Database Health, Payment System Health, Match Engine Health, Search Performance, Property Verification Health, Daily Vacancy Confirmation Health, Notification Delivery Health, AI Operations Health and API & Integration Health.

## AI Self-Healing

Routine issues such as failed notification delivery, background job failures, temporary synchronisation problems, receipt regeneration, cache refresh, search indexing delays, temporary API interruptions and minor AI processing failures are prepared for automatic recovery without interrupting the Founder.

## Founder Alerts

Only genuinely important issues reach the Founder, such as platform outages, payment gateway failures, security breaches, critical database failures, unrecoverable service failures and customer-impacting operational failures. Alerts are prepared for Founder Dashboard and WhatsApp.

## Business Opportunity Intelligence

The AI analyses customer searches to identify unmet demand, not just popular searches. It considers property category, property type, county, town, estate or neighbourhood, price range, search frequency, matching supply, verified supply and failed search count.

## Opportunity Queue and History

Unmet demand creates Founder Business Opportunities ranked as Critical, Growing or Emerging. Each opportunity keeps history so the Founder can see whether recruitment efforts solved, improved or left the opportunity unresolved.

## AI Business Intelligence and Health Reporting

The AI monitors market demand, property supply, customer behaviour, geographic trends, vacancy trends, registration growth, verification trends and platform performance. Reports summarise Platform Health, Operational Health, AI Performance, Payment Health, Search Health, Verification Health, Notification Health, Opportunity Queue and Opportunity Progress.

## Security

Monitoring data is protected and business intelligence is accessible only to authorised Founder administration. Customer information is not exposed unnecessarily.
