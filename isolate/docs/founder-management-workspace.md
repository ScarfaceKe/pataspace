# PataSpace Founder Management Workspace and AI Operations Centre

This module completes the Founder Administration Dashboard by adding the Founder Management Workspace and AI Operations Centre.

## Workspace layout

The workspace is organised into Dashboard, AI Summary, Approval Centre, Properties, Users, Verification, Payments, Viewings, Reviews, Notifications, Reports, Platform Health, Settings and Audit Logs.

## AI Summary Workspace

The AI Operations Officer prepares meaningful summaries for platform performance, verification progress, customer activity, property growth, payment performance, vacancy confirmation compliance, review trends, security observations and operational recommendations.

## Approval Centre

Only cases requiring Founder approval appear here, including verification recommendations, suspicious payments, reported reviews, suspected fraudulent accounts, security incidents, exceptional customer disputes and manual override requests. Each case contains a summary, timeline, supporting evidence, AI recommendation, confidence level and recommended action.

## Management workspaces

Founder workspaces are prepared for property management, user management, verification, payments, customer support, moderation, operational search, reports, platform health, settings and audit logs.

## Operational search

Universal search can locate properties, users, payments, reviews, viewing requests, notifications, verification cases and AI recommendations with direct management paths.

## Audit and security

Audit logs record Founder approvals, rejections, manual overrides, security actions, AI recommendations and system changes. Logs are searchable and protected from modification. The workspace uses the highest security and the AI Operations Officer cannot exceed authority levels from Master Prompt 22A.
