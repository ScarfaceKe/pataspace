# PataSpace Platform Health Operations Centre and Intelligent Incident Management

This module completes the Platform Health Monitor by implementing the operations centre, intelligent incident management, recovery history, live monitoring, AI diagnostics and long-term reliability monitoring.

## Operations Centre Dashboard

The Operations Centre displays Overall Platform Health Score, Individual Health Scores, Current Platform Status, Active AI Recovery Tasks, Active Critical Incidents, Open Business Opportunities, System Performance Summary and AI Operational Summary.

## Live Service Monitoring

The centre monitors Authentication, Property Registration, Property Verification, House Match, Shop Match, Office Match, Event Hall Match, Payment, Unlock This Listing, Verified Access, Viewing Workflow, Reviews & Ratings, Notifications, AI Admin Assistant, Platform Analytics and Revenue Intelligence.

## Incident Management

Incidents are classified as Minor, Moderate or Critical. Critical incidents are customer-impacting and require immediate Founder notification. Every incident records detection time, affected service, root cause where known, AI recovery actions, current status and final resolution.

## AI Recovery Centre and History

AI recovery actions include notification restart, receipt regeneration, background job restart, search index refresh, payment callback queue recovery, API connection recovery and database optimisation. Recovery history records incidents, actions, recovery time, resolution status, AI confidence and Founder intervention requirements.

## Reliability Analytics

The centre measures uptime, recovery success rate, average recovery time, incident frequency, service reliability, AI recovery effectiveness and stability trends.

## Founder Timeline and Diagnostics

The Founder Health Timeline shows critical incidents, successful recoveries, improvements, milestones, payment service events, growth events and business opportunity milestones. AI diagnostic reports explain slow services, failing processes, improving services, optimisation needs, bottlenecks and stability recommendations in non-technical language.

## Business Opportunity Progress

The centre integrates with the Business Opportunity Queue and shows opportunities created, active, solved, improving and requiring Founder attention.

## Security

Operational information is available only to authorised Founder administration. Recovery logs, diagnostics, health reports and incident history are protected and auditable.
