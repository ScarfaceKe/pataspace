# PataSpace Master Prompt 1 Foundation

## Permanent scope

PataSpace is a Kenya-only smart rental discovery platform. It is not an Airbnb clone, not a property-selling platform, and not a hotel booking platform.

## Supported property categories

Only these categories are supported:

1. Houses — single rooms, bedsitters, one bedrooms, two bedrooms, three bedrooms, four bedrooms, five bedrooms, and mixed residential properties.
2. Shops — different shop sizes and business suitability.
3. Offices — different office sizes and office types.
4. Event Halls — different hall capacities and event suitability.

No additional property category may be introduced unless a future approved master prompt explicitly specifies it.

## Kenya-only geography

All product language, workflows, and location logic are designed for Kenya. The location model supports country → county → town → area/estate/suburb → market centre/village, so all Kenyan counties and emerging urban areas can be represented without redesign.

Featured foundation locations include Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru, Kitengela, Machakos, Thika, Naivasha, Nyeri, Embu, Meru, Kakamega, Kisii, Malindi, and Nanyuki.

## Product philosophy

The product must feel simple, clean, fast, professional, modern, and easy for first-time users. Screens must be minimal, spacious, mobile-first, responsive, and clear.

## Invisible intelligence

Intelligence improves search quality, match quality, verification, notifications, reviews, admin assistance, and platform health behind the scenes. Customer-facing experiences must use structured guided interviews rather than open-ended AI chats.

## Trust principles

Trust is more important than quantity. The foundation encodes verified properties, accurate vacancy confirmation, transparent reviews, honest property information, verified property managers, clear payment flows, and reliable notifications as product guardrails.

## User roles

The foundation defines Customer, Property Owner, Property Manager, Leasing Agent, and Platform Admin for future role-specific workflows and dashboards.
