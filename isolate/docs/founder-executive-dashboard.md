# PataSpace Founder Executive Dashboard and Business Growth Intelligence

This module completes the Platform Analytics and Business Intelligence module.

## Executive KPIs

The Executive Dashboard displays Active Customers, Active Property Owners, Active Property Managers, Active Leasing Agents, Total Registered Properties, Total Verified Properties, Active Unlock purchases, Active Verified Access purchases, Pending Verifications, Pending Viewing Requests, Payment Success Rate, Daily Vacancy Confirmation Compliance and Platform Health Score.

## Business Growth Analytics

The dashboard displays daily growth including new customers, newly registered properties, newly verified properties, new Unlock purchases, new Verified Access purchases, completed viewings and reviews submitted today.

## Intelligence

The system prepares property performance intelligence, Event Hall intelligence, geographic intelligence, property category intelligence, customer behaviour intelligence and AI business intelligence. AI recommendations are recommendation-only and never change Founder-approved rules.

## Executive Reports

Founder reports are prepared for daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual and custom date ranges. Reports combine revenue, platform growth, customer activity, property performance, operational performance and AI executive summary.

## Personalisation and Security

The Founder can personalise KPI order, favourite widgets, default reporting period and preferred landing dashboard. Personalisation affects only the Founder dashboard and does not alter business logic. Executive analytics remain protected and only the Founder may access them unless explicitly authorised in the future.
