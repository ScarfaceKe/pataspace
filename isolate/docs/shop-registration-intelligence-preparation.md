# PataSpace Shop Registration Intelligence and System Preparation

This module extends the accepted Master Prompts 1–6A. It does not redesign Shop Registration, and it does not implement matching, verification, ranking, payments, reviews, notifications, AI Admin Assistant operations or Platform Health Monitor operations.

## Objective

Every successfully registered shop is automatically prepared for approved PataSpace systems without requiring duplicate setup after registration.

## Prepared systems

Each shop registration receives behind-the-scenes preparation for:

- Shop Match
- Vacancy Verification
- Search Priority
- Smart Rotation
- Match Results
- Unlock This Listing
- Verified Access
- Viewing Workflow
- Property Reviews
- Notifications
- AI Admin Assistant
- Platform Health Monitor

## Shop Match preparation

Shop Match preparation organises registration data for future matching using location, road visibility, business suitability, water availability, nearby places, vacancy information and real-world unit identification. No duplicate information is requested.

## Vacancy preparation

If vacant shop units exist, each vacant unit is prepared for publication and Daily Vacancy Confirmation. If no vacancies exist, the property remains registered but is not prepared for customer search visibility until vacancies become available.

## Search Priority preparation

The structure prepares approved future ranking signals: verified vacancy status, active vacancy confirmations, freshness of vacancy information and other Founder-approved search priority rules. Ranking is not performed in this prompt.

## Smart Rotation and Match Result preparation

Registered shops are prepared for future Smart Rotation, limited result batches, individual Unlock This Listing and Verified Access recommendations. These systems are not implemented here.

## Unlock This Listing and Verified Access preparation

Every shop unit inherits the official shop Unlock pricing key according to the approved pricing structure. Manual pricing entry is not allowed during registration. Verified Access recommendation logic is prepared but not executed.

## Viewing, reviews and notifications

Every shop is prepared for the approved Viewing Workflow contact methods: Request Viewing through the platform, Call the Property Manager or contact person, and WhatsApp the Property Manager or contact person. Shops are also prepared for future reviews and approved notification topics.

## AI Admin Assistant and Platform Health Monitor preparation

The AI Admin Assistant recognises newly registered shops for future verification support, vacancy monitoring, review prioritisation and platform recommendations. It remains invisible and never replaces the Platform Administrator.

The Platform Health Monitor can later analyse vacancy confirmation activity, search demand, areas with limited supply, properties awaiting verification and other Founder-approved recommendations. No registrant action is required now.

## Continuity

Future shop workflows must build on this preparation layer and must not require duplicate shop registration.
