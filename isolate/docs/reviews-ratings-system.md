# PataSpace Reviews & Ratings System

This module extends Master Prompts 1–17. It implements reviews for Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls.

## Eligibility

Customers may review only after a genuine interaction. Houses, Shops and Offices become review-eligible after a completed viewing. Event Hall reviews become available only after the booked event has taken place.

## One review per customer

Each customer may submit one review per property or vacant unit. Customers may edit their own review, and edit history is preserved for moderation.

## Ratings and categories

Every review includes a 1–5 star rating. Category ratings use simple toggles where relevant, including Accuracy of Listing, Cleanliness, Property Condition, Security, Accessibility, Value for Money, Customer Service, Communication and Overall Experience.

## Written reviews and responses

Written reviews are optional. Customers are encouraged to leave honest, respectful and helpful feedback. Property Owners, Property Managers and Leasing Agents may leave one professional official response visible with the review.

## Moderation and reporting

The system flags potentially offensive, spammy, promotional, duplicate or irrelevant reviews for moderation. AI Admin Assistant helps by flagging suspicious reviews but does not automatically delete legitimate feedback. Reports go to moderation and do not immediately remove reviews.

## Visibility and verified reviews

Public property cards can display overall star rating and total review count. Eligible reviews show verified interaction so customers know the reviewer completed the required property interaction.

## Review impact

Reviews contribute to property reputation and customer confidence. Reviews do not override existing Match Engine ranking rules unless a future Founder prompt explicitly approves that.

## Security

Customers may only review eligible properties and edit only their own reviews. Registrants may respond only to reviews associated with their own properties. Every review action is recorded.
