# PataSpace Brand Identity and Global Design Standards

This document extends the accepted Master Prompt 1 foundation. It does not replace any Prompt 1 scope, category, trust, location, role, or invisible intelligence rule.

## Official brand

The official platform name is **PataSpace**. The platform must not use alternative names or abbreviations.

## Brand position

**PataSpace is Kenya's smart rental discovery platform.**

PataSpace helps people find houses, shops, offices, and event halls through guided matching and verified rental listings.

## Target audience

PataSpace is designed exclusively for people searching for rentals in Kenya. Wording, layouts, workflows, locations, and examples must feel familiar to Kenyan users.

## Four official customer entry points

The primary customer entry screen must present exactly these four clean interactive cards:

1. 🏠 Find My Home
2. 🏪 Find My Shop
3. 🏢 Find My Office
4. 🎉 Find My Hall

These are the official entry points into the customer rental discovery experience. No additional entry points may be introduced unless specified in a future approved prompt.

## Global visual standards

Every screen should be clean, modern, spacious, fast, easy to scan, mobile-first, responsive, and accessible.

Use consistent spacing, typography, icons, and button styles. Avoid clutter and unnecessary choices.

## Interaction standards

PataSpace guides users one step at a time. Avoid long forms whenever possible. Approved interface patterns include cards, toggles, radio buttons, dropdowns, search fields, and simple selection interfaces. Sliders may only be used where previously approved.

## Adaptive interview principle

The platform does not ask every question. It adapts. Every answer determines which question appears next, and irrelevant questions must never be shown.

This applies across House Match, Shop Match, Office Match, Hall Match, and future guided workflows.

## Performance and feedback

Every interaction should feel responsive. Users should receive immediate visual feedback after actions such as saving, confirming, paying, requesting a viewing, and completing a workflow. Loading states must be clear but unobtrusive.

## Trust through design

Trust must be reinforced visually and functionally through clear verification badges, honest property information, transparent payment states, accurate review summaries, professional notifications, and simple confirmation messages.

## Error handling

User-facing error messages must be friendly, specific, and helpful. Technical errors must not be displayed to users. The interface should explain what happened and provide the next logical action.
