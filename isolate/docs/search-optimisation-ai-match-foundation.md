# PataSpace Search Optimisation and AI Match Engine Foundation

This module establishes the platform-wide Search Optimisation and AI Match Engine Foundation for Houses, Shops, Offices and Event Halls.

## Core philosophy

The Match Engine answers: Which available property best matches what this customer wants? Every ranking decision begins with the customer’s actual requirements. Match Accuracy is always the highest-ranking factor.

## Guided filters

Each property category has a category-specific search workflow using toggle buttons, option cards, multi-select choices and Yes / No switches. Only relevant filters appear for the selected category.

## AI Search Description

Customers can use the text area titled **Tell us more to help us find your ideal property.** The AI extracts useful preferences while preserving the customer’s original meaning and without restrictive character limits.

## Ranking priority

Priority 1 is Match Accuracy. Additional signals include verification, reputation history, reviews, water and electricity where applicable, road accessibility, parking, distance, nearby facilities and condition. Daily Vacancy Confirmation is the final ranking factor and must never outweigh Match Accuracy.

## Nearby alternatives

The engine searches the requested location thoroughly first, including supported estates and neighbourhoods. Nearby towns are suggested only when no meaningful matches exist and must be geographically reasonable.

## Saved searches and personal assistant

Customers can save searches with selected filters and AI Search Description. Saved searches are private, never bypass Customer Access Control, and may be monitored quietly for meaningful new matches. Notifications should be grouped when several similar properties become available.

## AI learning and security

AI may improve interpretation of customer descriptions but must never modify Founder-approved ranking priorities, Customer Access Control, pricing or business rules. Searches remain private and saved searches belong only to the customer who created them.
